TACTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Built For Zero-Connectivity Scenarios

Designed for disaster relief, remote hiking, crowded events, and resilient off-grid communication.

Direct Radio Links

No cellular towers, no Wi-Fi routers, no servers. Transmits direct peer-to-peer radio waves over 2.4 GHz Bluetooth Low Energy with custom GATT services.

RF Spectrum Radar

Real-time visual radar calculates signal strength (RSSI), categorizing nearby nodes into Immediate (<1m), Near (1-5m), and Mid-Range (5-15m) proximity.

Smart Phone-Only Filter

Advanced heuristics filter out Bluetooth TVs, set-top boxes, smart bulbs, and peripheral noise, ensuring your radar and contact lists display only actual phones.

Emergency SOS Alarms

3-second hold-to-activate distress beacon fires maximum-gain emergency pulses. Triggers high-priority notifications with Morse code audio and instant "Problem Solved" cutoff.

Heads-Up Chat Banners

System notifications alert you outside the app with sender callsigns and message previews, vibrating and deep-linking directly into direct peer chat conversations.

Cross-Platform Mesh

Standardized binary packet protocol allows Android and iOS devices to discover, link, exchange ACK receipts, and broadcast mutual emergency alarms seamlessly.

CROSS-DEVICE SUPPORT

Seamless iOS & Android Interoperability

Both Android (Kotlin / Jetpack Compose) and iOS (Swift / SwiftUI + CoreBluetooth) share identical 128-bit GATT UUID specifications and binary packet encoding.
